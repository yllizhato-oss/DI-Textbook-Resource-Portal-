ABOUT THE PORTAL

Supporting Future Educators

The DI Textbook Resource Portal was developed as a digital companion to the study entitled "Differentiated Instruction Strategies in Selected Teacher Education Textbooks at Mabalacat City College: A Content Analysis." It provides an organized space where future educators can explore selected textbooks, identified differentiated instruction strategies, and related learning resources.
